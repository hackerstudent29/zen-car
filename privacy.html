Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | Version 2.4

At ZEN Automotive Corporation ("ZEN", "we", "our", or "us"), our commitment to tranquility extends beyond mechanical engineering into how we safeguard your digital sanctuary. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information collected through our web experience, interactive vehicle visualizer, and reservation portal.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data footprint. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver our mindful automotive experience:

  • Contact Information: Full name, verified email address, phone number, and delivery jurisdiction when you request an allocation or reserve a vehicle.
  • Vehicle Preferences: Trim selection (GT-1, GT-P, GT-Zen), exterior/interior finish, and delivery configuration choices.
  • Anonymized Telemetry: Non-personally identifiable metrics on page performance, device viewport classification, and audio-visual playback capabilities to optimize rendering quality.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is utilized solely for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Processing vehicle reservations and coordinating production allocation slots.
  • Providing timely dispatch updates, technical bulletins, and private sanctuary viewing invitations.
  • Ensuring site security, enforcing terms of reservation, and mitigating fraudulent transactions.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We respect your privacy preferences. We employ standard essential cookies and local storage tokens strictly to maintain session integrity, preserve your cookie consent selection, and store your audio preference states. We do not sell your personal data or partner with cross-site behavioral tracking syndicates.

4. Data Retention & Safeguards

All sensitive information transferred through our reservation portals is encrypted using TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols with modern cipher suites. Access is limited to authorized production engineers and concierge managers. You may request data deletion or an export of your information at any time by contacting our privacy team.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, ZEN affords you fundamental data rights:

  • Right of Access and Portability: Request a structured copy of data associated with your reservation profile.
  • Right of Rectification: Modify inaccurate contact coordinates or reservation specifications.
  • Right of Erasure: Request complete deletion of your reservation inquiry before formal contract finalization.

6. Contact the Sanctuary Desk

For inquiries regarding privacy governance, data requests, or compliance, please reach out to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@zenautomotive.com.
